## 2. What it settles

- **At speed 50 with everything on, the main thread is ~60% sim, ~25% render, the rest dispatch.** The draw side's
  per-item transform block (idea 1) is small here (`GetInstanceTransform` is not in the top 60; `Lerp` + `AsMatrix` +
  `operator!=` ~1.5%): `render_culling` and `draw_gather` cut the draws, and the sim fills the frame. Idea 1's ~20% is the
  speed-10 uncapped faithful number and should be measured there (draw ms, both towns), not expected to show at speed 50.
- **The reference frustum cull is the largest single draw block in the everything-on profile (9.6% of main-thread ops)**:
  `ext.draw_gather` tests every box with `RtVolume_Frustum_Contains` and never reaches the prepared table. Idea 2
  (CULLROUTE) gets the `draw_gather.cpp:251` site as a first-class target (sent to the agent during the block).
- **`CharacterGrid::Place` at 6.5%** is the price of WALKBATCH's class A variant (Place every tick). The class B variant
  (`kPlaceEveryTick = false`, measured -3..-4% EntityList, unshipped at the user's call) is the one sim-side item this
  table ranks above everything else; it stays the user's decision, recorded here with its number.
- The walk itself (`ForEachWalkCandidate` + the lambdas + `Cached<MakeWalkTick>` ~14%) is instructions, not misses; the
  only sim rows with a miss rate are the prefetch stage (by design) and `DecideWalk` (0.4% of ops).
- The four path workers are nearly idle at speed 50 with `fast_paths` on; nothing to take there.
